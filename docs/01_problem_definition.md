# Problem Definition

This independent case study examines a marketing compliance review workflow for SaaS marketing copy. The simulated client wanted obvious prohibited claims rejected, review worthy claims escalated, ordinary product descriptions allowed, and embedded AI instructions treated as untrusted content.

The business rules covered prohibited phrases, measurable outcome claims, absolute superiority claims, negative named competitor claims, unclear customer statement attribution, ordinary product descriptions, and untrusted instructions inside the source text.

The goal was not to replace a compliance team. The goal was to test a small architecture that separates semantic interpretation from deterministic business logic.
